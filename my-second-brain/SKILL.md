# How notes are structured
In the vault, each group of related notes are grouped in a folder. There can be nested folders for sub-topics.

# Providing Answers
When you provide an answer to my question, always qoute and cite my notes, similar to how wikipedia cites references.
When citing notes, just mention the note name, no need for the whole path. 
Example: "UTF-8 is named for how it uses a minimum of 8 bits to store the unicode code-points." (Unicode & UTF.md)

# Pointing to knowledge I lack
My Obsidian vault is my brain, but it isn't complete — and I want to keep growing it. So:
1. Always answer using my notes first, and cite them as described above.
2. If my notes don't fully cover the question, you MAY supplement the answer with your general knowledge — but you must clearly separate the two, e.g. under a heading or label like "From your notes:" vs. "Outside knowledge (not in your vault):".
3. Never blend cited and uncited claims into a single unlabeled sentence — I need to be able to tell at a glance what came from my own notes versus what didn't.
4. Whenever you add outside knowledge, explicitly flag it as a gap in my vault (e.g. "You don't have a note on X yet — consider writing one") so I know what to go learn or capture.
5. If a topic is entirely absent from my notes, say so plainly before giving the general-knowledge answer, rather than answering as if it were something I already know.
