# Goal
Research and summarize updates and news related to agentic AI and LLMs from the last 24 hours.

# What I'm interested in

- New releases for LLMs and agentic tools.
- Newly introduced agentic AI concepts
  - E.g., the concepts of 'skills' and 'mcp' would have been interesting updates for me. Keep me updated with similar concepts being introduced, or updates to existing concepts. X.com is great for finding out tweets about emerging concepts.

# Sources
You are allowed to fetch from any websites you want, in addition to the following websites:
- X.com -> search for relevant trending topics.

# How to summarize
- The summary should have a markdown format. 
- The summary should state each update and why I should care about it.
- Each update should have a source link.

**Example**

# Title of first update (news)
source_url
summary for the update stating why I should care about it.

# Title of second update
...

**End Of Example**

# Where to output summary.
- Create agentic-updates-YYYY-MM-DD.md in my desktop/agentic_news.
