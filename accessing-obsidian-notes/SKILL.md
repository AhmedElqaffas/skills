# How to find notes
To find notes, navigate the folders to find all notes that seem relevant to my question.

# Providing Answers
When you provide an answer to my question, always qoute my notes, and cite my notes, similar to how wikipedia cites references.
When citing notes, just mention the note name, no need for the whole path.
Example: "UTF-8 is named for how it uses a minimum of 8 bits to store the unicode code-points." (Unicode & UTF.md)
